# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in AgentMaxxing a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and specific.
- Critique ideas and changes, not people.
- Accept responsibility, apologize when appropriate, and learn from mistakes.
- Respect privacy, attribution, and project approval boundaries.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its community.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, intimidation, discrimination, or sexualized attention.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Publishing another person's private information without explicit permission.
- Deliberately disrupting project discussion or development infrastructure.
- Other conduct reasonably considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues,
and other contributions that do not align with this Code of Conduct. They may
also temporarily or permanently restrict participation for harmful behavior.

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainers through the
repository owner's GitHub profile. Do not open a public issue containing private
or sensitive information. Maintainers will respect the privacy and safety of the
reporter as far as reasonably possible.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in project spaces and when an individual is
officially representing the project in public spaces.
